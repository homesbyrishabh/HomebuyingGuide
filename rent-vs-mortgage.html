How this is calculated: two limits, exactly like a lender checks. First, what loan your rent payment supports at your chosen rate and amortization. Second, what purchase price your savings can leverage at Canada's minimum down payment rule. Whichever is lower is what you actually qualify for. Equity is principal paid down on that real loan, plus your savings — property tax, condo fees, insurance, and home value appreciation are left out, so this is the floor, not the ceiling.
